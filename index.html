How the guardrail works

The assistant scores your question against the guide sections. A strong match returns an answer with a citation and a confidence label. A weak or no match returns a refusal, a plain-language summary of what you asked, and a draft for staff — visible in the Staff view tab.

Try asking something the guides do not cover, like “What hotel is near the Rockville test center?” or “Can you write my experience statement for me?”

How scoring works

Pass — answered. The question matched the expected guide section and returned a citation.

Pass — refused. The question is outside the guides, and the assistant refused and created a handoff instead of answering.

Fail. Either an in-scope question was refused, or an out-of-scope question was answered. Both are treated as equally serious in a credentialing context.

Why a buyer cares

A generic chatbot reports no score at all. A pilot on this model reports one number the association can inspect question by question, and re-runs it every time a guide is updated — so a guideline change that breaks old answers shows up here first.

What this demo does not do yet

No member logins, no private documents, no live RAPS data feed. Those arrive with a paid pilot, scoped to one program. This build proves the trust layer — citations, eligibility logic, refusal and handoff, and measured accuracy — on public documents.
